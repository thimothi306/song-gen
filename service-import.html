Import a service list

Paste a service plan or upload an image. The importer reads the text and separates the date, song list, message, and offering into editable fields. Check the result carefully—especially Telugu OCR—before saving.

1. Add your list

Image recognition runs in your browser using Tesseract.js and downloads the Telugu/English language data when needed. Internet access is required for the first OCR run.

2. Review and edit

Open song sheet generator

This first step saves an editable service-list draft in this browser. It does not search for or download lyrics yet; that will be the next step after you confirm the extracted song names.
